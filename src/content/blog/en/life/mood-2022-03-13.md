## 13 March, sunny

I spent two hours today clearing the room and threw out a bag of useless things: expired instructions, half-written notes, three charging cables that will never find their other halves again.

Oddly, the moment I sat down afterwards, a part of my head was empty too. Many of those “things I never finish” were just clutter on the desk reminding me they existed.

**The messier it is outside, the noisier it is inside.** Order can start with the small square you can reach — tidy one drawer and you have one less corner of distraction.

> When your mind is a mess, tidy one drawer first. Cheapest therapy there is.

The bookshelf tomorrow.
