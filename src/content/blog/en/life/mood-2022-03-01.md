## 1 March, sunny

A new page rarely begins at the moment you feel ready.

I meant to read today. I sat down at the desk, turned seven pages, and drifted off three times. Once I would have called that “not being in the right state”. Today I took it differently: **seven pages still count.**

Ritual is the excuse we look for before starting; real beginnings are plainer than that — open the book, put your shoes on, open the file. You don’t need a lucky hour, and you don’t need your mood to arrive first.

> Do the thing once today. If it goes badly, do it again tomorrow. Starting never needs a ceremony — it only needs today.

March, one step forward.
