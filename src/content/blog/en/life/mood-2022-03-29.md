## 29 March, sunny

I ran a small experiment today: the phone went into another room, and I kept one whole hour for myself.

The first ten minutes were uncomfortable; my hand kept reaching for a pocket. After twenty, my head began to quieten. After forty, I read the hardest pages of this stretch of weeks. So I never lacked time — I just kept cutting it into scraps.

**Attention is the most expensive thing there is right now.** If you don’t protect it, someone else will spend it for you.

> Putting the phone further away works better than forcing yourself to “focus”.

That hour was worth it. Again tomorrow.
