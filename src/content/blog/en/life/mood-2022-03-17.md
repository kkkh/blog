## 17 March, cloudy

The word “persistence” is said too forcefully, as if it required gritted teeth every single day.

My experience is the opposite: **the things I have actually kept up are the ones I made light enough** — twenty pages a day, three kilometres a day, three hundred words a day. Light enough that skipping them feels wrong.

Fervour fades; habits stay. A person running on enthusiasm usually gets outwalked by a person running on habit.

:::warning
**Don’t use willpower, use rules.** Willpower runs out. Rules are still there tomorrow.
:::

> Lower the bar until it is almost laughable, then step over it every day.

Done, today.
