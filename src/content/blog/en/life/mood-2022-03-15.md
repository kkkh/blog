## 15 March, sunny

The person I worked alongside today is far better than I am. Watching him work, I had a moment of feeling small, and a moment of wanting to step away.

But I stayed. Not out of stubbornness — I worked something out: **you improve fastest where you feel stupid.**

Around people weaker than you, it is easy to be comfortable and easy to stand still. Around people stronger than you, it is uncomfortable, but every day forces a little growth. Discomfort and growth are often two sides of one thing.

> Don’t fear being behind. Fear being somewhere no one makes you want to catch up.

I asked three questions today that may have sounded naive. Worth it.
