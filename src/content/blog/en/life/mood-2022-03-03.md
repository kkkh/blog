## 3 March, cloudy

I got up an hour earlier than usual today. It wasn’t quite light; a street-cleaning truck passed outside, and the corridor was very quiet.

I didn’t use that hour for anything big: boiled water, washed my face, finished the few pages I had left from yesterday. But the whole day felt different — as if it started with me leading it rather than being pushed along.

**Time isn’t squeezed out, it’s woken up.** The two extra hours you borrow from the night have to be paid back the next day; the hour you win in the morning is yours.

> The first hour of the morning is the only hour that belongs entirely to you.

Tomorrow, again.
