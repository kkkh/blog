## 5 March, windy

I ran three kilometres today for the first time. Slowly — I even stopped and walked twice. My legs were heavy at the end, but something in me was bright.

I have no intention of comparing paces with anyone. Someone else’s five kilometres has nothing to do with me; my three have something to do with me — last week I couldn’t finish one.

**Slow doesn’t matter; still moving doesn’t matter either.** The hard part is not going faster — it is putting your shoes on again tomorrow.

> Progress is not speeding up. It is not stopping that day.

A note for whoever I am later.
