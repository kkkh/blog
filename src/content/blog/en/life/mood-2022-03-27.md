## 27 March, night

The lamp is on; unfinished things are spread across the desk. There have been many nights like this.

One thing I keep telling myself: **staying up late is not praiseworthy in itself.** It is good that the world goes quiet at night — but if I spent the day putting things off, then what I burn at midnight is the day’s debt.

Every minute ground out at night is not wasted — but **hours taken at night are borrowed from tomorrow, while hours earned in daylight are truly yours.** The same two hours are called efficiency in the morning and overdraft after midnight.

> The light at midnight is beautiful — better if it falls on devotion rather than procrastination.

Sleep early tonight.
