## 31 March, sunny

March is over. I went back through the month’s notes: one day I drifted off after seven pages, one day I finished three kilometres, one day I was turned down, and a few heavy evenings.

Imperfect. But compared with February I did a few more things, and understood myself a little better.

**The point of looking back is not to count results; it is to know which way to go next.** Keep what worked, find the reason for what didn’t, then carry on.

:::success
**Three things March gave me**

- [x] A start (even from a very low point)
- [x] A morning habit that lasted most of the month
- [x] The ability to say “I haven’t learned that yet”
:::

> Even an imperfect thirty days deserves a note — at least it proves you were moving.

See you in April.
