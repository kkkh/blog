## 8 April, sunny

After twenty scattered days of writing, I asked myself the most basic question: why bother trying?

Not so that someone will look up to me, and not so I can match someone else’s picture of a good life. The plainest answer is — **so that I have a choice.**

Someone capable of things can choose to stay or to leave; can choose to do a thing or to refuse it. Effort never buys honour; it buys options. The more options you have, the calmer you become.

:::info
**The point of effort is to turn “I have to” into “I can”.**
:::

> Win the right to choose back into your own hands, inch by inch.

Twenty essays, and this is the one I stop at.
