## 23 March, sunny

There was something today I didn’t understand. I hesitated for two seconds, then said it: “I haven’t learned that yet.”

Saying it was a relief. I used to nod along, then quietly catch up on my own later — no loss of face on the surface, and one wasted chance to solve it right there.

**Admitting you don’t know is the first step to knowing.** Pretending to know is exactly why you stay ignorant. Nobody expects you to know everything; but everyone remembers the person willing to start from the beginning.

> “I haven’t learned that yet” takes more courage than “I’ve got it” — and it’s worth more.

One less pretense today.
