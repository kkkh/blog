## 21 March, sunny

Today I noticed that the whole row of trees along the road had turned green. Last week they looked bare; I have no idea which night it happened.

Spring does not arrive on one particular day; it arrives day by day — a little more green, a little more warmth, an inch more light. By the time you can see it, it has been going for a long while.

**Everything is quietly growing, and so are you.** The effort that “shows no results yet” is probably accumulating somewhere you cannot see, simply not yet at the point of budding.

:::info
**Not visible does not mean not growing.** Before the flower opens, the root keeps going down.
:::

> Don’t rush to prove anything. Grow quietly first.
